# G15 candidate prompt set

Generated with the built-in imagegen workflow, one call per icon, with genuine transparent
background requested for every call. Shared direction: isolated 128×128 production UI icon;
polished hand-painted dark-fantasy mobile-game finish; strong simple silhouette; midnight
navy/deep-purple shadows; warm antique gold trim; restrained magical rim light; centered
square composition; generous safe padding; no badge/card background, text, letters, numbers
or watermark; readable at 24–40 CSS px.

## Per-icon subjects and constraints

- **Announcements:** curled parchment announcement scroll plus a small herald trumpet; no
  price or currency symbol and no real-money implication.
- **Guide:** open ancient book with blank ivory pages, dark leather cover, gold corner trim
  and a subtle purple magical bookmark/rune glow.
- **Terms:** upright rolled legal parchment with a prominent round purple wax seal and short
  ribbon; no price or currency symbol.
- **Privacy:** sturdy closed purple-and-gold padlock with a clear keyhole; no chain or shield.
- **Fair Play:** compact purple knight shield with exactly one bold gold check mark; no cross
  or swords.
- **Purchase:** curled receipt-like policy document with a small gold circular return arrow;
  no readable text, currency sign, coin, gem, shopping cart, credit card or price tag, and no
  implication that real-money checkout is active.

High-resolution generated masters were uniformly trimmed, resized within a 104×104 content
box, centered on a transparent 128×128 canvas, and stripped of metadata. No candidate was
repainted during normalization.
